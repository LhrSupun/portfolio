01

about

I’m an engineer who enjoys the whole stack — from query planners and queue back-pressure to ROS2 nodes, flight controllers, and the layout grids and empty states on top. My day-to-day lives mostly between TypeScript, Python and Postgres, with cloud (Azure, Kubernetes) and robotics (ROS2, ArduPilot) close behind.

I care a lot about maintainability: clear modules, honest types, and tests that fail for the right reasons. I think good software is mostly good taste applied consistently over time.

Off the keyboard, I read a lot, drink too much tea, and tinker with small side projects — some of which even ship.
