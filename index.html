01

about

I’m an engineer who enjoys the whole stack — from query planners and queue back-pressure to ROS2 nodes, flight controllers, and the layout grids and empty states on top.

Right now I’m a Senior Robotics System Integration Engineer at Xavier AI in Colombo, working on robotics system integration and design. I got here through full-stack work: a vehicle-routing module at EIS, game backends and player subscription systems at NineHermits, and a fire-safety inspection app prototype at Frontwalker.

My day-to-day lives mostly between TypeScript, Python and Postgres. On the web that usually means MERN (MongoDB, Express, React, Node), with cloud (Azure, Kubernetes) and robotics (ROS2, ArduPilot) close behind.

I care a lot about maintainability: clear modules, honest types, and tests that fail for the right reasons. I think good software is mostly good taste applied consistently over time.

Off the keyboard, I read a lot, drink too much tea, and tinker with small side projects — some of which even ship.
